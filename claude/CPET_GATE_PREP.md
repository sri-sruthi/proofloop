## Tiered plan (assuming aggregate-ish; adjust when you get the pattern)

### Tier 1 — highest ROI (Monday focus): Aptitude & Logical Reasoning
Most trainable fast + **overlaps TCS Sunday** (one prep, two gates).
- Quant: Numbers (divisibility, HCF/LCM, power cycles), Percentages (P&L, SI/CI),
  Ratio/Proportion/Averages, Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance, Data Interpretation.
- Reasoning: Arrangements/Seating, Blood Relations, Direction Test, Syllogisms, Series,
  Odd-man-out, Coding-Decoding, Clocks & Calendars, Data Sufficiency.
- Resource: **Feel Free to Learn** (shortcuts, TCS-pattern, TN-friendly). Watch a method →
  immediately drill timed questions. Watching ≠ preparing.

### Tier 2 — quick wins (Tuesday evening): Verbal
Fast points, overlaps TCS verbal.
- Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary, Grammar (3 Qs), Analogy, Critical Reasoning,
  Statements & Assumptions, Syllogism, Logical Ordering of Sentences.
- Resource: **CareerRide** (stronger on verbal + interview content).

### Tier 3 — play to strength, DON'T cram (fit around Tiers 1–2): Technical
You're a final-year DS student — much of this is recall, not new learning.
- **Lead with strengths (free marks):** AI/ML/GenAI (your projects), Python.
- **High-yield quick brush-up:** SQL basics (joins, group by, subqueries), DSA basics
  (arrays, strings, sorting, Big-O), DBMS basics.
- **Skim for awareness only:** OS & Computer Networks common facts, Cloud fundamentals
  (= the AWS you're already learning for Aivar), Software Eng practices (Agile/Git).
- **Ignore unless pattern forces it:** Full-stack, DevOps/CI-CD, Cybersecurity,
  System Design/LLD — too broad to cram; skim titles for recognition at most.

## Synergy to remember
- Tiers 1–2 ARE your TCS prep — zero waste.
- Tier 3 Cloud + AI/ML overlaps your Aivar study — they reinforce each other.

## Method discipline
- Learn a method from video → drill timed questions on it → move on. Solving under the
  clock is the prep; passive watching is not.
- Breadth over depth this week: competent across the cutoff beats perfect in one area.
- Tuesday night: REST. A sharp brain Wednesday beats one more cramming hour.
